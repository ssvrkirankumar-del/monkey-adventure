## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

This comprehensive quality audit assesses every environment visual asset currently used or available in **Level 01 (The Awakening)** against our cinematic production benchmark.

Assets are rigorously classified into four categories:
- **A. KEEP**: Production-ready, meets cinematic visual bar without changes.
- **B. UPGRADE**: Structurally sound, but requires higher-resolution PBR textures (2K/4K), micro-detail normal maps, roughness variation, or multi-LOD geometry optimization.
- **C. REPLACE**: Below benchmark quality (e.g., flat geometry, visibly procedural/synthetic noise, basic low-poly approximations). Requires dedicated photorealistic models.
- **D. IGNORE / REMOVE**: Obsolete placeholder assets, primitive blocks, or low-poly starter kit remnants.

## 4. Top 5 Critical Upgrades for Level 01

1. **Ground / Path Geometry Replacement**: Replace flat primitive box tiles with organic sculpted ground terrain featuring natural elevation curves, dirt trail blending, exposed tree roots, and mossy rock embankments.
2. **Canopy Tree PBR & Foliage Shading Upgrade**: Upgrade `HD_Tree_JungleCanopy_01` and `HD_Tree_CoconutPalm_01` with 2K photorealistic bark PBR textures, alpha-cutout leaf cards, and two-sided foliage lighting with sub-surface scattering.
3. **Heavy Stone Door & Ancient Ruin Masonry Sculpting**: Replace primitive box door slabs with carved monolithic ancient temple doors featuring recessed celestial rune glyphs and eroded stone edges.
4. **Cinematic URP Lighting & Volume Post-Processing**: Configure warm tropical morning sun angles with soft contact shadows, depth fog, SSAO (Screen Space Ambient Occlusion), and ACES filmic color grading.
5. **Photogrammetric Rock Surface Detailing**: Integrate 2K multi-frequency rock normal maps and triplanar top-surface moss blending across `HD_Rock_MossyBoulder_01` and `HD_Rock_Cliff_01`.
